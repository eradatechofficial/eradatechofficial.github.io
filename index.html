Arrow Dock

Spot the arrow. Fill the dock.

About the game

Tap an arrow whose path off the board is clear and it flies into the dock. Three of a colour clear together. The dock only has six slots, so the order you release arrows in is the whole puzzle. Fifty levels across five chapters add hidden, frozen, locked and long arrows.
